# Mecasimetra Verified Business Network

**Company:** Mecasimetra Systems & Kappology  
**Status:** Commercial product specification  
**Date:** 2026-08-20

## Identity

A verified business receives the Mecasimetra **magenta pine** verification mark.

The mark means the account has satisfied Mecasimetra's current verification criteria. It does not imply government certification, financial solvency, product endorsement, or regulatory approval.

Verification can expire or be revoked when eligibility, account status, or verification evidence changes.

## Social design

Mecasimetra's business network is designed for utility rather than engagement maximization.

A business may publish a post of up to **100 characters**.

The only engagement action on a post is:

```text
SHARE
```

The initial design intentionally excludes:

- likes;
- comments;
- replies;
- public reaction totals;
- follower counts;
- popularity rankings;
- engagement scores; and
- algorithmic pressure to post frequently.

## Business benefits

### Verified discovery

Businesses may opt in to a verified-business directory. Discovery should prioritize explicit filters and relevance rather than popularity metrics.

### Shareable milestones

A business may choose to share approved milestones externally, including customer-authorized statements about qualified savings, service improvements, product launches, or company updates.

Private Meca Verify measurements remain private unless the account explicitly selects an approved value for sharing.

### Team and workspace controls

Business tiers may support:

- workspace members;
- role-based access;
- usage and budget permissions;
- notification roles;
- report/export permissions;
- billing-view permissions; and
- future payout-approval permissions.

### Business record

Meca ID should eventually provide one account record for:

- verification status;
- tier;
- pricing schedule;
- promotion status;
- invoices and credits;
- Meca Verify reports;
- workspace roles;
- approved posts;
- service history; and
- customer-authorized wallet settings.

### Reporting

Businesses may export approved customer-facing reports for accounting, procurement, management, and operations. Exports must not reveal Mecasimetra proprietary routing, Kappology implementation, private security controls, or another customer's information.

### Premium eligibility

Verified businesses may be eligible for:

- Founding Premium or other promotions;
- lower performance-fee schedules;
- advanced Continuity/Signal controls;
- longer reporting retention;
- multiple workspaces;
- priority support;
- early access to eligible Edge/Replay capabilities; and
- business-specific onboarding.

Eligibility is a product/commercial decision and is not guaranteed solely by verification.

## Privacy

Directory participation is opt-in. Private savings, billing, usage, wallet balances, provider selections, internal account risk information, and treasury data are not published by default.

## Moderation

Even 100-character posts require protection against impersonation, scams, malicious links, harassment, unlawful content, and disclosure of secrets. Public text must be escaped/sanitized and subject to reporting, suspension, and verification review.

## Pine mark usage

The magenta pine mark is a Mecasimetra source-identifying verification symbol. Public use should be limited to accounts and surfaces actually authorized by Mecasimetra. The mark should not be presented in a way that falsely implies government or independent third-party certification.
