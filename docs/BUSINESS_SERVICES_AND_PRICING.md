# Mecasimetra Business Services & Pricing

**Company:** Mecasimetra Systems & Kappology  
**Status:** Commercial pricing draft for testing  
**Date:** 2026-08-19

## Pricing principle

Mecasimetra should charge for customer-visible service and measurable value rather than obscure markups.

Provider/inference costs remain separate from Mecasimetra service fees where practical. Performance fees apply only to contractually defined qualified value or savings.

## Initial service ladder

### Meca Verify — Entry

**Target:** individuals, developers, and small teams evaluating Mecasimetra.

Proposed price:

- $0 platform fee during evaluation period;
- provider/inference cost at actual applicable rate;
- up to 20% performance fee on qualified savings where enabled;
- limited retention and reporting;
- one account/workspace.

Purpose: minimize adoption friction and establish an auditable baseline before asking the customer to buy a larger license.

### Meca Business Core

**Target:** small businesses and production AI teams.

Proposed standard price:

- $49/month platform license;
- provider/inference costs separate;
- 15% performance fee on qualified savings;
- Verify dashboard;
- Prime managed optimization;
- Continuity monitoring;
- Signal notifications;
- basic budget controls;
- standard support.

### Meca Business Premium

**Target:** businesses that want the complete Meca control surface and stronger support.

Proposed standard price:

- $149/month platform license;
- provider/inference costs separate;
- 12.5% performance fee on qualified savings;
- all Business Core capabilities;
- longer reporting/ledger retention;
- advanced Continuity and Signal policies;
- multiple workspaces/projects;
- priority support;
- early access to eligible Meca Edge/Replay features;
- eligibility for promotional rate protection.

### Meca Enterprise

**Target:** larger organizations, privacy-sensitive deployments, private infrastructure, or high-volume usage.

Proposed pricing:

- custom platform fee;
- lower negotiated performance fee where volume justifies it;
- private deployment/integration options;
- custom retention and data controls;
- organization-level policy management;
- dedicated support;
- optional service-level agreement only where supported by evidence and contract.

Enterprise remains a negotiated commercial agreement rather than a click-through promotional license.

## Founding Premium Promotion

Early business accounts may be offered **Founding Premium** status.

Recommended initial offer:

- one-time Premium activation/license fee: **$199**;
- promotional Business Premium platform rate: **$59/month**;
- promotional qualified-savings fee: **10%**;
- promotional term: **12 months** from activation;
- provider/inference costs remain separate.

The one-time activation fee gives Mecasimetra immediate working capital while keeping the recurring service materially below the proposed standard Premium rate.

## Promotion renewal

Founding Premium is a promotion attached to an otherwise standard Business Premium license.

At the end of each promotional term, Mecasimetra may:

1. renew the promotional rate for another defined term;
2. offer a different promotion;
3. move the account to the then-current standard Business Premium schedule; or
4. allow the customer to downgrade or cancel.

Mecasimetra should not call the promotion a permanent or lifetime price lock if the company intends to retain renewal discretion.

During an active price-protection term, the published protected price should not be changed except for clearly disclosed circumstances such as taxes, third-party pass-through charges, customer-requested plan changes, abuse, nonpayment, or another condition expressly stated before purchase.

## Why separate license from promotion

```text
BUSINESS PREMIUM LICENSE
    = product capability and permissions

FOUNDING PREMIUM STATUS
    = temporary promotional pricing entitlement
```

This separation lets Mecasimetra evolve the product license without pretending every early customer has a perpetual commercial rate.

## Pricing comparison context

The initial schedule is intentionally positioned below many mature AI-observability/team platforms while Mecasimetra proves its own production value. Pricing should be reviewed after real customer usage, support cost, provider cost, conversion, retention, and verified-savings data exist.

## Required billing disclosures

Before purchase, the customer should be shown clearly:

- platform/license fee;
- whether the charge recurs and how often;
- provider/inference costs;
- performance-fee percentage and calculation basis;
- promotional start/end date;
- standard price after the promotion, if known;
- whether promotional status may be renewed;
- cancellation process;
- taxes or pass-through charges where applicable; and
- what happens when the customer changes tiers.

No preselected paid add-ons or hidden recurring charges.

## Adjustment authority

Mecasimetra may change standard pricing prospectively. Existing active promotional terms remain governed by the promotion terms presented to that account.

Pricing experiments should be versioned so the company can reconstruct what a customer saw and accepted at signup.
