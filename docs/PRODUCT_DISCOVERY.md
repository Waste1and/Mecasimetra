# Mecasimetra Product Roadmap — Public

**Company:** Mecasimetra Systems & Kappology  
**Status:** Product strategy  
**Date:** 2026-08-19

## Product thesis

Mecasimetra is building a unified customer system around measurable computational outcomes, secure control, minimal data collection, and proprietary systems intelligence.

The public product family is:

```text
Meca ID -> Verify -> Prime -> Continuity -> Signal
                         + Edge / Replay
```

## Meca ID

Minimal account identity, optional customer-controlled wallet linking, notification preferences, and product access.

## Meca Verify

Customer-facing measurement and reconciliation for contractually defined service outcomes and attributable value.

Potential commercial models include subscription, fixed audit/service fees, usage pricing, or performance-oriented pricing where the customer agreement provides a clear and reproducible basis.

## Meca Prime

Managed computational optimization for supported AI workloads under customer-defined quality and service requirements.

The proprietary decision process is not part of the public specification.

## Meca Continuity

Operational monitoring and change detection for supported systems. Customer-facing output should focus on understandable state, trends, and alerts while internal detection methods remain private.

## Meca Signal

AI-assisted product, operational, and security notifications tied to authorized account events and customer settings.

## Meca Edge

Mecasimetra-hosted inference for bounded workloads where testing supports the product's published service characteristics.

## Meca Replay

Scenario analysis, systems review, and service-assisted evaluation for operational and infrastructure decisions.

## Commercial sequence

1. establish Meca ID and customer controls;
2. establish Verify and billing integrity;
3. introduce Prime behind a stable service contract;
4. connect Continuity and Signal;
5. add Edge where production evidence supports it;
6. offer Replay/enterprise systems work;
7. extend into additional company domains as validated products emerge.

## Revenue surfaces

Possible revenue models include:

- subscriptions;
- metered service usage;
- contractually defined performance fees;
- systems audits and Replay engagements;
- enterprise/private deployment;
- hosted inference; and
- support/security/operations services.

Pricing must not reward degraded customer quality, unnecessary traffic, hidden markup, or misleading baselines.

## Public evidence rule

Confidential experiments, private benchmarks, internal algorithms, formulas, calibration, model-selection logic, and research methods are not published merely to prove that the company has proprietary technology.

Public claims should rely on evidence deliberately approved for disclosure and appropriate to the claim being made.
