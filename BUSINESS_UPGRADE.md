## Kappology Research Principle

The recurring systems hypothesis is:

> Fragmentation → Drift → Waste → Sparse Control → Continuity

Kappology does not assume that one equation automatically transfers across every domain. Domain-specific observables, units, thresholds, baselines, and validation procedures are required.

The generalized pentagonal function

`P(k) = k(3k - 1) / 2`

remains a candidate research geometry. It must compete empirically against alternative models rather than being assumed universal.

## Evidence Standard

Mecasimetra public material should distinguish four levels:

1. **Defined** — a concept, metric, or framework has a precise definition.
2. **Implemented** — working code or an executable artifact exists.
3. **Experimentally observed** — a documented experiment produced a reproducible result.
4. **Validated** — independent or sufficiently rigorous evidence supports broader use.

Performance numbers, uptime claims, accuracy claims, throughput claims, years-of-experience claims, and client outcomes should not be published without supporting evidence.
