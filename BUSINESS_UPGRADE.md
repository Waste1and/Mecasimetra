# Mecasimetra Systems & Kappology — Business Upgrade 2026

## Legal identity

**Mecasimetra Systems & Kappology** is the company name used throughout the professionalization program.

## Business direction

Mecasimetra is being developed as a multidisciplinary systems company. Kappology is the company's research field. Public company materials describe product capabilities and research domains without disclosing confidential methods, formulas, benchmarks, algorithms, datasets, or implementation details.

## Company domains

### Computing & AI
AI infrastructure, software systems, orchestration, automation, model services, data systems, security, and edge computing.

### Agriculture
Research and future product work may include sensing, resource optimization, automation, crop-system monitoring, and resilient farm infrastructure.

### Biomedical Systems
Research may include biomedical computation, systems modeling, health-data methods, diagnostics research, and medical technology. Biomedical work requires independent scientific validation and applicable ethical, privacy, safety, and regulatory review before clinical or patient-facing use.

### Energy & Infrastructure
Energy efficiency, cooling, telecommunications, data-center systems, resilient networks, and infrastructure monitoring.

### Industrial Systems
Automation, manufacturing systems, logistics, machinery monitoring, and operational resilience.

## Meca System

The initial commercial architecture is organized around:

- Meca ID;
- Meca Verify;
- Meca Prime;
- Meca Continuity;
- Meca Signal;
- Meca Edge; and
- Meca Replay.

The public experience should operate as one control surface. Proprietary intelligence remains behind documented product interfaces.

## IP standard

Public repositories must not contain confidential research methods, internal formulas, private benchmark results, optimization mechanisms, model-selection logic, security thresholds, fraud/abuse logic, confidential datasets, or other trade-secret material.

Public documentation may describe what a product does, what data it uses, what outcome the customer receives, how a charge is reconciled, and what customer controls exist.

## Evidence standard

Public claims must be appropriately supported. Internal research evidence is not automatically approved for public disclosure. Performance numbers, uptime, accuracy, throughput, savings, medical outcomes, regulatory status, or customer outcomes must not be published without an appropriate evidence and disclosure review.

## Legal and compliance standard

- Use the company name consistently in legal-facing documents.
- Do not describe a technology, token, network, medical product, wallet feature, or service as exempt, compliant, approved, safe-harbored, or non-regulated without a documented basis and appropriate review.
- Separate software licensing from regulatory conclusions.
- Maintain privacy, security, wallet/payment, sanctions/export, biomedical, AI, and other relevant reviews as products evolve.
- Keep research statements separate from commercial warranties.
