# Intellectual Property Policy — Mecasimetra Systems & Kappology

**Status:** Public ownership and disclosure policy  
**Date:** 2026-08-20

## 1. Ownership principle

Original works created for Mecasimetra Systems & Kappology are owned by the applicable author or company rights holder, subject to any employment, contractor, contributor, assignment, license, or other written agreement that legally controls.

This policy does not claim ownership of third-party code, established mathematics, public standards, upstream software, external models, external datasets, or other material owned by others.

## 2. Public licensed work

Files intentionally distributed under Apache-2.0 or another public license remain available under the permissions granted by that license. Copyright ownership and licensing are separate: granting a license does not by itself transfer copyright ownership.

Public release should therefore contain only material the company is willing to license under the applicable public terms.

## 3. Proprietary and confidential work

The following are private by default and are not licensed merely because public Mecasimetra materials describe related products:

- unpublished Kappology research;
- proprietary algorithms and optimization methods;
- private benchmark construction, datasets, results, and analysis;
- internal mathematical formulations and calibration;
- model-selection logic, evaluator logic, internal prompts, weights, and training methods;
- proprietary model assets;
- customer-specific policies and confidential telemetry;
- security, fraud, abuse, and anomaly-detection methods;
- private datasets and data-processing logic;
- treasury, signing, authentication, and operational secrets; and
- other non-public trade-secret or confidential material.

## 4. Trade-secret handling

Material intended to remain a trade secret should be kept in private repositories and systems, access-controlled on a need-to-know basis, excluded from public issues and logs, and covered by confidentiality obligations where employees, contractors, partners, or vendors receive access.

Trade-secret protection depends in part on reasonable efforts to preserve secrecy; the company should not rely on labels alone.

## 5. Brands

Mecasimetra Systems & Kappology claims rights, to the extent protectable under applicable law, in its source-identifying company/product names and branding. Use of ™ indicates a trademark claim and does not imply registration. The ® symbol should not be used absent a valid registration for the relevant mark and jurisdiction.

## 6. Contributors and contractors

Substantial external work should not be incorporated into the proprietary core without a written agreement addressing ownership or license of contributed work, pre-existing/background IP, confidentiality, patent rights where relevant, third-party/open-source provenance, and authority to contribute.

## 7. Evidence and timestamps

Git history, dated research records, signed releases, laboratory records, and internal reports can help establish chronology and provenance, but they do not by themselves create a patent, trademark registration, or resolve every ownership dispute.

## 8. Patents and public disclosure

Potentially patentable inventions should receive patent-strategy review before public disclosure because publication can affect patent rights.

## 9. Public disclosure rule

The public repository should explain products, customer outcomes, legal boundaries, interoperability, and trust controls without disclosing private implementation methods unless an explicit disclosure decision has been made.

See `docs/PUBLIC_PRIVATE_BOUNDARY.md`.

---

This policy is an internal/public governance framework, not legal advice. Formal ownership, assignments, registrations, and patent strategy should be reviewed by qualified counsel where commercially important.
