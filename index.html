Trust architecture

Privacy, security, and authorization are product features.

01

Minimal data

Collect only what is needed to provide, secure, measure, support, and bill the selected service. Customer production content is not training data by default.

02

Self-custodial by design

Wallet linking can establish wallet control without requiring seed phrases or private keys. Asset-moving actions remain customer-authorized in the initial architecture.

03

Separated authority

Site, security, research, release, and treasury automation are designed with separate roles and limited permissions.

04

Private implementation

Public interfaces explain customer outcomes. Confidential research, security logic, and proprietary implementation remain private.
