Public research note

Kappology

Kappology is the systems-research field developed within Mecasimetra Systems & Kappology.

It studies continuity, change, control, and system behavior across computational and other domains. Public materials intentionally describe the field at a high level.

Proprietary mathematics, benchmarks, algorithms, calibration, optimization methods, internal datasets, and unpublished research remain within the private research boundary.

Public vocabulary

What customers may see.

These terms describe product concepts, not disclosure of internal mechanisms.

CONTINUITY

Required behavior over change.

Used publicly to describe persistence of service or system behavior as conditions change.

DRIFT

Meaningful change from baseline.

A general systems concept for changes in workload, quality, behavior, or operating conditions.

CONTROL

Deliberate system action.

Customer-facing products may recommend or apply authorized actions while internal decision methods remain proprietary.

Disclosure boundary

Research stays protected.

The public website is not a publication archive for confidential Kappology work. Technical disclosure requires an explicit company decision and appropriate evidence review.
