## Accounts

Meca ID may support a username, short description, optional wallet linking, product status, and notification preferences. Users must not place private keys, seed phrases, API secrets, unlawful content, or confidential third-party information in public profile fields.

Customer asset-moving transactions require customer review and authorization through the applicable wallet flow unless a separately reviewed product expressly establishes another arrangement.
