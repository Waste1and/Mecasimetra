# Privacy — Mecasimetra Systems & Kappology

**Status:** Draft public summary for product development. Requires legal review before production launch.

## Minimal-data approach

Mecasimetra products are designed to collect only the information needed to provide, secure, measure, support, and bill the selected service.

The initial Meca ID account design may include a username, short description, optional wallet identifier, account/product status, and notification preferences.

Profile pictures are not part of the initial design.

## Wallet information

Wallet linking is designed around customer-controlled/self-custodial wallets. Mecasimetra does not request or store wallet seed phrases or private keys through normal product flows.

A wallet address may become linked to an account and should not be treated as anonymous merely because it is publicly visible on a blockchain.

## AI and usage data

Product services may process operational metadata such as account identifiers, service/provider identifiers, usage counts, service cost, status, latency, billing-policy identifiers, and customer-visible outcome measurements.

Raw prompt and response content is not a default billing requirement. Customer production content is not model-training data by default.

## Notifications

Customers may receive product, operational, and security notifications according to product requirements and their notification settings. Notifications should contain only the information needed to explain the event.

## Retention and security

Production datasets must have a documented purpose, retention rule, access boundary, and deletion process. Sensitive data should be protected using appropriate access controls, encryption, secret management, and logging.

## Regulated features

Certain wallet, payment, biomedical, or other regulated products may require additional information, verification, eligibility controls, or jurisdiction-specific notices. Such information should not be collected merely for a basic Meca ID account.

## Contact

Until a dedicated privacy channel is published, privacy questions may be directed through the official Waste1and/Mecasimetra GitHub project.
